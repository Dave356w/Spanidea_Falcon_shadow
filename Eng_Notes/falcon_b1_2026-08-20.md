# B1 — the latch now says where it happened, and it found a false departure on boot

**Bench, 2026-08-20**, same session as `falcon_session_a_2026-08-20.md`.
Test plan §3 (B1); instrumentation gap 1. Flash **31550 → 31900**, 356 free.

---

## 1. What the line says

Every departure latch now emits exactly one line, whatever caught it:

```
FSM: Departure latched (polled) ml=33 q=1 dq=0 td=-1
```

| field | meaning |
|---|---|
| `(path)` | `any-motion`, `polled`, `velocity` or `unknown` |
| `ml=` | ms since `STATE_MONITORING` was entered |
| `q=` | `lat_monitor.quiet_run()` at the latch |
| `dq=` | any-motion edges the re-arm blank discarded this episode |
| `td=` | ms since the last discard, or −1 if there was none |

**How to read it for the failure it exists to catch:**

- `dq=0` — nothing was thrown away, so this latch is the first motion evidence
  of the episode → **departure at the start of travel**.
- `dq≥1` with `td` seconds ago — an edge was discarded and the latch came much
  later → the discarded edge was the real departure and **this latch is the
  stop**. That is the missed-departure signature, and it is exactly the
  reconstruction the 2026-08-18 session did by hand (4 of its 6 missed runs
  carried an `any-motion ignored, re-arm blanking` line for their departure
  edge).

**Why not the obvious signals.** Cruise is invisible on this device by
construction, gap 3 records that lateral `m` sits at rest levels through a
20 fpm run, and time in MONITORING is park + travel when the departure is
missed. None of the three separates the two cases. What was *discarded* does.

**Emitted at the state transition, not at the three latch sites.** That is the
one choke point every path passes exactly once, and it also closes the half of
gap 4 that mattered most: the polled path used to enter `MOVEMENT_DETECTED`
**printing nothing at all**, so any run count taken from `Departure latched`
silently undercounted — and it undercounted exactly the runs the backstop
caught. `latch_path` is first-setter-wins because the polled branch has no
`break` and falls through into the other two in the same pass.

## 2. 🔴 What it found immediately: a false departure on a stationary bench

First boot after flashing, device sitting still, nobody touching it:

```
FSM: Departure latched (polled) ml=33 q=1 dq=0 td=-1
```

**3 of 4 boots, and the numbers are identical every time.** This is the latch
that appeared and disappeared during session A and could not be attributed then
— the instrument named it in one boot.

| | |
|---|---|
| path | **polled**, not any-motion |
| `ml=33` | **33 ms after entering MONITORING** |
| `dq=0` | the re-arm blank is not involved at all |
| threshold | `0.040000` on every boot — clamped to `Z_THRESH_MIN` |

### 2.1 🔴 The polled departure path has no re-arm blanking

The blank lives in `MovementService::notify_any_motion()`. **The polled test in
`fsm_run()` is not gated by it, or by anything else.** The instant
`STATE_MONITORING` is entered, `delta_accel > threshold_value` is live against
an average that was refilled from `zero_calib_value` milliseconds earlier and is
still settling.

That is what `ml=33` means, and it is why the 6000 ms window the 2026-08-07
session added never protected against this: it protects one of the two paths.

Two things make it reachable rather than theoretical:

1. **`threshold_value` clamps to `Z_THRESH_MIN` (0.040) in every mounting tested
   so far** — the 08-18 note says both of its mountings clamped, and all four
   boots here did too. The polled threshold is sitting on its floor everywhere
   it has been measured, so there is no learned headroom above the settling
   transient.
2. **The average genuinely moves at that moment.** Measured earlier the same
   day: `avg` 9.748 → 9.695 against a `zero_calib` of 9.748, a delta of 0.053
   against the 0.040 gate. 1.3×, on a device at rest.

**Failure direction: a false departure — the beacon asserting over a stationary
car**, released only by `LATCH_FAILSAFE_MS` or by an unrelated disturbance. On a
counterweight at commissioning it would present as the unit alarming the moment
it finishes calibrating.

### 2.2 Why it is intermittent, and why session A's A/B was confounded

1 of 4 boots did not latch, with the same clamped threshold and an
indistinguishable average. The condition is marginal, and the margin moves with
the mounting's settling — which is why the same phenomenon read as 4/4 against
0/4 across two time-separated arms during session A, and 3/3 clean on both
builds an hour later. Recorded in `falcon_session_a_2026-08-20.md` §3.

**Not fixed here.** B1 is instrumentation; the fix is a design decision. The
obvious candidates — gate the polled path on the same re-arm blank, or hold it
off until `quiet_run()` has climbed once after calibration — both change
departure sensitivity, which is the one direction §14.4 calls catastrophic. It
wants Dave's call and a bench run of its own.

**All nine on the polled path. Seven of nine latched inside the re-arm blank,
`ml` 541–1556 ms against a 2500 ms floor.**

The loop is closed and self-feeding:

1. Latch → beacon.
2. The beacon's own mechanical transient, plus the stop ringing, reaches
   `ARRIVAL_PEAK_VALUE` → release.
3. → `STATE_MONITORING`.
4. The polled test is live **immediately**, the ringing has not decayed, and
   `delta_accel` clears a threshold clamped at 0.040 → **re-latch at ~600 ms**.
5. Back to 1.

`FSM: re-arm blank cleared early` appears **once in the whole sequence**, because
in every other cycle the unit re-latched before the blank could finish. The
blank is not failing — **it is not in this path.**

### 2.3a This is the 2026-08-07 failure, and the fix for it covers one path of two

`MONITOR_REARM_MS` was added on 2026-08-07 to stop exactly this: "prevent one
trip alarming twice", after a terminal-floor brake set re-latched 3.5 s into
MONITORING and alarmed for 73 s over a stationary car. That fix has been in
place ever since **in `notify_any_motion()` only**.

**What changed, and why it is surfacing now:** the polled path used to be
effectively unreachable. `DEFAULT_THRESHOLD_VALUE` was 0.40, and the 08-18 note
measured its reachability at **1 of 82 departures**. Since the self-calibrated
threshold landed (08-18 §5), `threshold_value` is learned and **clamps to
`Z_THRESH_MIN` 0.040 in every mounting measured** — a 10× sensitivity increase
on the one departure path that has no re-arm gate. A path that almost never
fired now fires first, every time.

`q=0` on most of these latches is the device stating that its own lateral has
not settled. The information needed to reject them is present and unused.

### 2.3b Fix direction

Gate the polled test on `rearm_cleared`, exactly as `notify_any_motion()` does.
All seven in-blank latches fail that test; `ml=117899` and `ml=2850` still pass.
It reuses machinery already trusted for the other path and adds no new state.

✅ **APPLIED 2026-08-20 on Dave's instruction.** The polled test now carries the
same `!rearm_cleared && elapsed < MONITOR_REARM_MS` condition as
`notify_any_motion()`. Suppressions print and count:

```
FSM: polled ignored, re-arm blanking t=16 d=0.0484
```

Rising-edge guarded — the test runs every `fsm_run()` pass and a ringdown holds
delta above threshold for hundreds of them. Flash **32176, 80 free**.

**The cost, stated plainly:** a genuine departure inside the blank is now missed
by this path too — the §14.4 catastrophic direction. It is accepted because
any-motion, the path that actually catches slow departures, has been blanked in
exactly this window since 2026-08-07. The change makes the two paths consistent
rather than opening a new blind spot, and the window can only shorten
(`rearm_cleared` latches as soon as the lateral is measured settled; observed
2507–2589 ms).

### 2.3c Verification, and what it does NOT establish

⚠️ **Six clean boots at the shipping threshold are NOT the evidence.** The
excursion measured only **0.026–0.039** on those boots, against a 0.040 gate —
it never reached the threshold, so the new branch was never reached either.
Reporting "6/6 clean, fixed" would have repeated §3's error exactly.

**What does establish it:** `Z_THRESH_MIN` temporarily forced to 0.015 to make
the boot excursion reliably exceed the gate, then four boots:

| boot | line |
|---|---|
| 1 | `polled ignored, re-arm blanking t=16 d=0.0393` |
| 2 | `polled ignored, re-arm blanking t=16 d=0.0357` |
| 3 | `polled ignored, re-arm blanking t=16 d=0.0398` |
| 4 | `polled ignored, re-arm blanking t=16 d=0.0484` |

4/4 suppressed, no latch, exactly one line each (the rising-edge guard holding).
`t=16` is the same instant the unfixed build latched at `ml=33`.

**Two of those excursions — 0.0484, and 0.0398 — reach or sit on the shipping
0.040 gate.** So on those boots the unfixed shipping build would have produced a
false departure, and the gate suppressed it. That is the fix working at the
shipping threshold, not only at the forced one.

`Z_THRESH_MIN` was restored to 0.040 and the shipping build re-flashed and
re-checked: `Threshold-Value : 0.040000`, clean boot, blank cleared at 2556 ms.

⬜ **Still unverified:** a `dq=1` *display*. The counter increments inside the
verified branch, but no latch followed a suppression in any capture, so the
non-zero value has never been printed.

⬜ **The cascade of §2.3 has not been re-run against the fix.** It needs a tap.
All seven of its in-blank latches (`ml` 541–1556) fall inside the new gate by
inspection, but that is inspection, not measurement.

## 3. What is not yet verified

⬜ **The `dq`/`td` half is UNEXERCISED, and §2.3 shows why it may stay that
way.** Every capture reads `dq=0` — including nine latches produced on demand.
The polled path latches *first* in every case, so no any-motion edge is ever
offered to the blank for it to discard. **The discard counter cannot count while
an ungated path beats it to the latch**, which makes fixing §2.3b a
prerequisite for validating this half rather than an unrelated task. No capture today
reached the discard branch at all — the boot-time any-motion edge arrives
*before* `monitor_entered_ms` is set, so the MONITORING entry block clears
`any_motion_pending` and the blank never sees it. Exercising it needs an edge
arriving inside the blank: tap the unit within ~2.5 s of `READY`, then again
after, and confirm the second latch reports `dq=1` with a plausible `td`.

⬜ **The failure it was built for has not been reproduced with it fitted.** The
missed departure is a 17–35 fpm car event; the bench cannot produce one. B1
earns its keep on the next slow-run session, not here.

## 4. Compatibility

`graph/session_g.py` classified latches by the presence of
`FSM: Departure latched` and inferred "polled/silent" from a `STATE_MOVING`
transition with no latch line. It now reads the stated path where the device
gives one and keeps the inference for captures that predate B1. Verified
against the committed corpus: **263 / 3 / 7 and the label census unchanged**.

`parse_falcon_log.py`, `session_d.py` and `velocity_replay.py` all match on the
`FSM: Departure latched` prefix, which is unchanged, so they are unaffected.
